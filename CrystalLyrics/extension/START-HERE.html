Free Chrome & Edge extension · Version 1.4

CrystalLyrics

Word-by-word lyrics with your colors, fonts and glow. Pause, skip, replay — the words follow your video.

Updating an older version? Copy these files into your existing CrystalLyrics folder, click Reload on the extension page, then refresh YouTube.

Install in Chrome

  1. Right-click the ZIP → Extract All. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Type chrome://extensions into Chrome’s address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the upper right.
  4. Click Load unpacked. Select the CrystalLyrics folder with manifest.json inside.
  5. Refresh YouTube. Open a song and look for the lyrics panel in the lower right.

Using Edge?

Use edge://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then select Load unpacked and the same folder.

No EXE, server, subscription, login or API key is needed. This is a desktop browser extension, installed as an unpacked folder.

If the panel is missing

Refresh the YouTube tab after installation. Click the extension’s ♪ icon and choose Show lyrics. Standard YouTube and YouTube Music watch pages are supported.

Your lyric controls

Style: lyric colors & glow

Choose independent colors for the active word, finished words, upcoming words and glow. Adjust Word glow from 0 to 100. It is steady, without flashing. Your choices apply to both boxes.

Style → Lyric effects

Toggle karaoke fill, word glow, word lift, word emphasis, word underline, line fade and soft focus independently. Combine your favorites; choices are remembered. The same controls and a still preview are in the extension popup.

Reduce motion keeps lift and emphasis still and makes fades instant. Turn it off for gentle movement. Effects follow playback, so pause and seek stay aligned. Reset effects restores just the effect switches.

Use cover art colors

Turn on this toggle in Style or the extension popup. It looks up a matching song cover and uses its palette for your words and glow. The status shows the matched album. If artwork is unavailable, your manual colors stay in use.

Use video colors

Turn on this separate toggle to match the video’s picture. Colors update gently every 2 seconds, and ads are skipped. One matching mode is active at a time. Switch it off to restore your saved manual colors. Some players block video color sampling; cover matching is available instead.

Fonts, transparent boxes, moving & resizing

Choose a bundled font or load your own. Set each background to 0% for full transparency. Drag the panel’s title bar and pull its corners to resize. Turn on Edit video box to move and resize the video lyrics. Your layout is remembered.

Find song

Search the actual artist + song title. Choose the matching version if automatic lookup was uncertain.

Click a lyric line

Jump the video to that line’s real timestamp. Scroll freely to browse; click Follow playback to return.

On video

Show the current and next lyric on the video, including fullscreen. Minimize the floating panel with its − button.

Load lyrics

Import LRC, enhanced LRC, SRT, VTT or a timed Lyricsfile YAML. Your imported file stays on this browser.

Fix an intro or timing mismatch

Lyrics show too early?

Press +0.25 to show them later.

Lyrics show too late?

Press −0.25 to show them earlier.

Video adds an intro?

Open More timing controls. Press First line starts now when you hear the first lyric.

The upload itself was sped up / slowed down?

Adjust Lyric speed in More timing controls. Leave this at 1.00 when simply using YouTube’s playback-speed setting.

Timing and your selected recording are remembered separately for each video.

Better word timing from the audio

  1. Load the correct song’s lyrics. Click the extension icon → Local audio timing → Learn this song.
  2. The video restarts at normal speed. Keep the song tab open and let it finish, or click Finish & analyze for the captured portion.
  3. Wait for local analysis, then replay when the status says timing is saved. First use downloads an approximately 80 MB model; processing time depends on your computer.

Audio stays on your device. The tool listens to this tab, not your microphone. Singing recognition can miss or mistime words; missed words keep the automatic estimated effect. Click Cancel to stop learning.

Know which timing you’re seeing

WORD SYNC uses supplied word timestamps. AUTO WORDS estimates word positions within actual line timings, so line-only songs animate immediately. AUDIO WORDS uses locally recognized word positions. Mixed labels appear when estimates fill gaps.

Automatic effects are estimates; audio recognition cannot guarantee perfect singing timing. Supplied word timestamps take priority. Turn off Automatic word effect if you want line-only highlighting. Untimed text remains clearly labeled. Ads suspend highlighting until the song resumes.

Live versions, remixes, video cuts and community lyric errors may need another recording or an imported file. Reduced motion is on by default. Chrome / Edge 116 or newer is required.
